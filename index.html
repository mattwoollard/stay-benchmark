How to read this

  • A night counts as booked when it can no longer be booked online. A night still for sale the day before is counted as unsold.
  • Long stretches that look like owner blocks are left out of the percentages.
  • Rates are per night. Airbnb rates are the all-in guest price, including cleaning and Airbnb fees. Properties marked "rate excl. cleaning" show the nightly rate from their own booking site.
  • Booked rate is the last price seen while each night was still for sale. Nights booked before tracking began use the property's average price.
  • Fleurieu Peninsula properties have been tracked since 6 November 2026, so their history and lead times will build up over the coming weeks.
